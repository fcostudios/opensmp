# 14 — Legal & Compliance Note: Ledger (`fcostudios__smp`)

**Step:** 14 — Legal · **Date:** 2026-07-22 · Internal group tool; scope deliberately thin. **This is an engineering-drafted note, not legal advice — route items 1–2 to counsel/HR before go-live.**

1. **Employee activity monitoring (the one substantive item).** Ledger ingests per-user, per-day vendor activity (chat/code sessions) and surfaces inactivity to admins/approvers. Under Ecuador's data-protection regime (LOPDP) and labor norms this is personal-data processing of employees: (a) document a lawful basis + internal privacy notice to the 30 companies' staff (what is collected, why — seat stewardship, not performance evaluation); (b) purpose-limit in policy AND product: activity data drives reclamation only (BR-16 already forbids automated action on activity alone; BR-15 requires human consent); (c) retention: define a horizon for ActivityRecord/CostRecord (proposal: 24 months, matching the perf fixture) — currently unspecified → **OQ-LEG-1 for counsel**; (d) access minimization is already structural (company scoping, aggregates-first per 03 SEC12 note).
2. **Statements are not invoices.** Non-goal preserved end-to-end: exports are labeled reconciliation-grade internal documents; no tax fields, no SRI e-invoicing obligations triggered. If external clients are ever formally billed (R3+ MSP), that decision reopens this note (facturación electrónica, retention rules).
3. **Vendor terms.** Automation uses Anthropic's documented Admin/Analytics APIs within published rate limits; seat purchases stay in the vendor UI (their contractual surface). No scraping, no ToS-adverse automation. Keycloak/pg-boss/Drizzle/Lucide/Barlow: OSS licenses (Apache-2.0/MIT/OFL) — compatible with internal use; @react-pdf MIT.
4. **Security posture as compliance floor.** Encrypted secrets (ADR-13), TLS, mandatory 2FA for admins (ADR-03), immutable audit (BR-03/28), break-glass audited — adequate for internal data; SOC 2-oriented controls remain R3+ per PRD §6.
5. **Cross-border:** data stays on the self-hosted VPS (location = OQ-SMP-7 decision input — prefer in-country or contractually adequate hosting for LOPDP simplicity).

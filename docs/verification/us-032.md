## Delivered contract

`saveRateCard` accepts manual exact decimal USD rates for a vendor account/license pair, inclusive from/to dates, and provisional evidence (default true). Optional id edits an unconsumed row. Global database-resolved Central Finance and Group Admin roles may read/save; company-scoped roles cannot access this global catalog. Every successful mutation and audit commit atomically.

PostgreSQL enforces no-overlap under concurrent writes, valid dates/nonnegative rates, and consumed-row update/delete locks. Pool locks serialize edits with existing or newly materialized final/reconciled consumers. Matching legacy statement lines without dates use the statement month. `/tarifas` renders rates and purchased-capacity history together, with manual form, edit controls, provisional/contracted labels, loading, empty and retry states.

## Strict immutability interpretation

BR-21 explicitly makes a consumed row immutable. No future-tail exception was authorized. A consumed open-ended row therefore cannot be shortened or replaced by an overlapping successor. Use finite periods established before finalization when successors are required; unconsumed provisional rows can still be corrected. The asynchronous policy question did not grant approval to relax this rule. This is a known consequence of the written optional-end-date/immutable-row contract, not a claim that provisional data passes real-data or H2 acceptance. Contracted inputs, real invoice and human production verification remain slice acceptance inputs under DEC-SMP-066.

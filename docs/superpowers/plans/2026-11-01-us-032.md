**Tech stack:** Next.js 16, Drizzle, PostgreSQL, React.

## Contract and review focus
saveRateCard accepts optional id (edit before final consumption), vendorAccountId, licenseTypeId, monthlyRateUsd (exact decimal string), effectiveFrom, nullable effectiveTo (inclusive), provisional (default true). It returns the persisted row. Unknown fields and invalid dates/precision reject. New periods never silently rewrite old rows. Locked corrections require a new non-overlapping period; an open-ended consumed row cannot be truncated. Database constraints protect concurrent overlaps. Final/reconciled statement lines identify consumed rates by pool and intersecting dates, falling back to statement month for legacy lines without dates.
